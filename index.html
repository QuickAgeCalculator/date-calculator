What is a Date Calculator?

A date calculator (also called a date difference calculator or days between dates calculator) is a free online tool that quickly computes the exact number of days, weeks, months, and years between any two calendar dates. It can also add or subtract a specific number of days from a given date and calculate business days by excluding weekends.

Whether you need to know how many days are left until a deadline, the duration of a project, your exact age in days, or the number of working days between two dates, a date calculator online free tool like this one gives you accurate answers in seconds.

How Date Difference is Calculated

The calculator treats both dates as pure calendar dates (time-of-day is ignored). It uses the actual Gregorian calendar, correctly handling leap years (February 29 in years divisible by 4, with century exceptions).

  • Total days – Absolute difference in days between the two dates. Optionally includes the end date (+1 day).
  • Years / Months / Days breakdown – A calendar-accurate decomposition that accounts for varying month lengths and leap years.
  • Weeks + remaining days – Total days converted into full weeks and leftover days.
  • Business days – Counts only Monday–Friday, skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

All calculations run entirely in your browser, so results appear instantly with no server round-trip.

Examples

Days between dates

Leap year example

From 1 February 2024 to 1 March 2024 = 29 days because 2024 is a leap year.

Add days to date

Business days

From Monday 3 March 2025 to Friday 14 March 2025 = 10 business days (weekends excluded).
